THE WORKFLOW

From email to resolution.

A simple flow for customers, agents and support administrators.

01 Receive

A customer emails your support mailbox or submits a portal request.

02 Route

DeskTicket creates a ticket, applies SLA rules and routes it to a team or agent.

03 Work

Agents reply, add internal notes, change status and collaborate around the request.

04 Resolve

Track SLA performance, resolve the request and keep the full conversation history.
